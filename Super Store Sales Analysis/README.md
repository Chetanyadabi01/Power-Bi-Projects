# 📊 Super Store Sales Analysis | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** built to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regions, shipping modes, and state-level performance. The project also includes a **15-day sales forecast** using Power BI's built-in forecasting capability.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Super Store sales data from **January 2019 to December 2020** to identify sales and profitability trends and provide actionable business insights through interactive Power BI dashboards.

The dashboard helps understand:

* Overall sales and profit performance
* Monthly sales and profit trends
* Category and sub-category performance
* Regional and state-level sales
* Customer segment contribution
* Shipping mode distribution
* Payment mode analysis
* Product-level performance
* Sales forecasting for the next 15 days

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that enables users to:

* Monitor key sales and profitability KPIs
* Identify high-performing and underperforming products
* Analyze sales trends over time
* Compare regional and customer-segment performance
* Understand shipping and payment patterns
* Identify areas requiring further investigation
* Forecast short-term sales performance

## 🔄 Work Done

### 1. Data Preparation

* Imported the Super Store dataset into Power BI.
* Cleaned and transformed the raw data using Power Query.
* Checked data types and prepared fields for analysis.
* Created calculated measures using DAX.

### 2. KPI Analysis

Created key performance indicators including:

* **Total Sales:** ~$1.57M
* **Total Profit:** ~$175.26K
* **Total Quantity:** 22.32K
* **Unique Orders:** 3,003
* **Unique Customers:** 773
* **Average Delivery Time:** ~3.93 Days

### 3. Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales and profit trends across months to identify:

* Monthly performance patterns
* Revenue fluctuations
* Profitability trends
* Peak sales periods

### 4. Product Analysis

Analyzed performance across:

* Categories
* Sub-Categories
* Products

The analysis identifies **Office Supplies** as the highest-sales category and **Phones** as a leading sub-category by sales.

### 5. Regional & Geographic Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across:

* Regions
* States
* Geographic distribution

The **West region** records the highest sales, while **California** is the highest-sales state in the dataset.

### 6. Customer & Shipping Analysis

Analyzed:

* Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments
* Shipping modes
* Payment modes

The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of sales, while **Standard Class** represents the largest shipping-mode share.

### 7. Sales Forecasting

Created a separate **15-Day Sales Forecast** page using Power BI's built-in forecasting functionality.

* Forecast period: **15 days**
* Confidence level: **95%**
* Historical sales data used to estimate short-term future sales.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Office Supplies** generates the highest sales among the major categories.
* **Technology** generates higher profit than Furniture despite having lower sales than Office Supplies.
* The **West region** records the highest sales contribution.
* **Consumer** is the largest customer segment by sales.
* **Standard Class** is the most commonly used shipping mode.
* **Phones** are among the highest-selling sub-categories.
* **California** records the highest state-level sales.
* **December 2020** records the highest monthly sales in the analyzed period.
* **Tables** show negative overall profit and require further investigation to understand the underlying causes.
